About J Rose Property Group

With over 20 years of experience in the building trade, J Rose Property Group brings extensive hands-on knowledge to every project. We cover all aspects of property renovation, from refurbishment and repairs through to transforming tired properties into attractive, finished homes.

Our focus is simple: Refurbish • Improve • Increase Value. We combine practical building experience with a clear understanding of what makes a property work, whether the goal is to improve an existing home or develop a property investment opportunity.
